Eight directions for the Record page, drawn with leo's own stylesheet. Each card shows today's page beside one idea. Red boxes on the after picture mark every change, numbered to the list under the card. Click a picture to flip between before and after.

A · One page, three tabs

The recording looks like the note it becomes: title, chips, then tabs for your notes, the transcript and what you want. Felix answers in his own panel.

    B · Notepad first

    You write the page; leo listens. Your notes are the page in the note font, the transcript runs in a narrow column, and the controls sit in one bar at the bottom.

      C · Who said what

      The transcript is the centre of the page, grouped into turns. Each voice gets a tone dot and a name you can change once for every line.

        D · One compact card

        Today's pieces, kept, in one card that fits a small window. A segmented control switches between your notes, the transcript and Felix.

          E · Felix in the margin

          Leo's own idea, not the reference app's: Felix reads along the live transcript the way he reads a note, and leaves short margin notes on what matters.

            F · Before you start

            The start screen gets the title first, smaller kind choices and the same red Start, so the page you set up is the page you record on.

              G · While the notes are written

              After Stop the page stays. Your notes and the transcript stay readable while Felix writes, and a third tab fills in when he is done.

                H · Speakers after recording

                The fixes from the speaker comparison below, on the transcript sheet of a call. Today the sheet shows time and text only; who spoke is saved but not shown.
